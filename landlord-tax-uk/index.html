UK landlord expenses and the GBP 1,000 property allowance

General information, not tax advice. Confirm on gov.uk.

Property allowance

If your gross rental income is GBP 1,000 or less in a tax year it may be covered by the property allowance. Above that you can usually deduct either the flat GBP 1,000 allowance or your actual allowable expenses, whichever is better for you.

Commonly allowable expenses

  • Letting agent and management fees
  • Repairs and maintenance (not improvements)
  • Landlord insurance
  • Utilities, council tax and service charges you pay
  • Ground rent
  • Legal and accountancy fees for the letting
  • Advertising for tenants
  • Travel to the property for the letting business

Mortgage interest is treated differently (a basic-rate tax reduction rather than an expense). Check gov.uk.

Keep simple records

Log each rent payment and each cost by property, date and category, so the year-end return takes minutes. The UK Landlord Rental Income Tracker does this in Excel or Google Sheets, compares the allowance with actual expenses and estimates the tax to set aside.

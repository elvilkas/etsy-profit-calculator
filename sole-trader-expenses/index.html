UK sole trader allowable expenses checklist

General information, not tax advice. Costs must be wholly and exclusively for the business; mixed private use usually needs splitting. Confirm on gov.uk.

Commonly allowable

  • Stock and materials you resell or use
  • Office costs: stationery, phone and internet (business share)
  • Travel: fuel, parking, public transport, or simplified mileage (45p / 25p per mile for cars and vans)
  • Working from home: flat rate by hours or a share of actual bills
  • Advertising and website costs
  • Software and subscriptions
  • Professional fees such as an accountant
  • Bank and payment fees, insurance
  • Equipment (usually through the annual investment allowance)

Usually not allowable

  • Everyday commuting
  • Clothes for normal wear
  • Fines and penalties
  • Entertainment of clients
  • Your own wages or drawings

Keep it organised

Record each cost by date, supplier, amount and category as you go. Do not mix simplified mileage with actual vehicle costs.

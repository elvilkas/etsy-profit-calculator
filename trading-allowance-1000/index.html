GBP 1,000 trading allowance: allowance or expenses?

General information, not tax advice. Confirm on gov.uk.

If your gross trading income is GBP 1,000 or less in a tax year, it is generally covered by the allowance and may not need reporting. Above that you can either deduct the flat GBP 1,000 allowance from your income or deduct your actual expenses. You pick one, whichever leaves less profit.

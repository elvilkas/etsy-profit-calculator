Working from home flat rate calculator (UK sole traders)

Self-employed people can claim a flat monthly amount for using their home as a base instead of tracking each bill. The simplified rate is based on hours worked at home each month: GBP 10 for 25-50 hours, GBP 18 for 51-100 hours, GBP 26 for 101 hours or more. Under 25 hours does not qualify for the flat rate. Check gov.uk for the current figures.

What to keep

Note the hours you work at home each month. You can still claim a proportion of actual costs instead if that is higher and you can show how you worked it out.

A ready-made tracker

The UK Sole Trader Self Assessment Tracker has a Home Office sheet that turns your monthly hours into the flat-rate amount and feeds it into the tax summary, alongside a mileage log and income and expense logs. Excel and Google Sheets.

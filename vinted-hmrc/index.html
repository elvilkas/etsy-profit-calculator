Do I have to tell HMRC about my Vinted or eBay sales?

Short answer: it depends on whether you are trading or just clearing out your own things. This page explains the basics and gives you a quick checker. It is general information, not tax advice; always confirm on gov.uk.

Selling your own unwanted items

Selling personal belongings you no longer need (a few clothes, an old games console) is generally not treated as trading, so there is usually no income tax to declare.

Buying to resell, or selling regularly

If you buy things to resell, make items to sell, or sell regularly to make a profit, HMRC is likely to see this as a trade. The GBP 1,000 trading allowance means that if your total trading income for the tax year is GBP 1,000 or less, you generally do not need to register or report it. Above that, you usually need to register for Self Assessment and report your profit.

What platforms report

Online platforms such as Vinted, eBay and Depop now collect and pass seller information to tax authorities, generally for sellers with 30 or more sales or about EUR 2,000 of income in a year. Being reported does not by itself mean you owe tax, but HMRC can see the numbers, so keep your own records.

What records to keep

For each sale: date, platform, amount and fees. For stock: what you paid and when. Also postage, packaging and any other costs of selling. A simple spreadsheet is enough as long as it is complete.

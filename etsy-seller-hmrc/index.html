Etsy seller tax in the UK: do I need to register with HMRC?

General information, not tax advice. Confirm details on gov.uk.

Hobby or trade?

Making and selling items occasionally, with no plan to make a profit, can be a hobby. Selling regularly, buying or making stock to resell, or advertising your shop usually points to a trade.

The GBP 1,000 trading allowance

If your total trading income for the tax year (before costs) is GBP 1,000 or less, you generally do not need to register or report it. Above that, you usually register for Self Assessment and report your profit, either deducting your real costs or using the allowance instead, whichever leaves less taxable.

Costs sellers commonly count

  • Materials and stock
  • Etsy listing, transaction and payment fees
  • Postage and packaging
  • Advertising, including Etsy Ads
  • Software and equipment used for the shop
  • A fair share of home-working costs (flat rate or actual)

Records to keep

Every sale (date, amount, fees), every cost with a receipt or statement, and the total for each tax year. Etsy and payment statements help but are not a full record on their own.

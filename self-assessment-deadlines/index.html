Self Assessment deadlines for sole traders and side hustlers

General information, not tax advice. Dates are for a tax year ending 5 April; confirm on gov.uk.

Key dates

  • 5 October after the tax year ends: register for Self Assessment if you are new to it.
  • 31 October: deadline for a paper tax return.
  • 31 January: deadline for the online return and for paying the tax owed (and the first payment on account, if you make them).
  • 31 July: second payment on account, if you make them.

Do you need to file at all?

If your self-employed or side income is under the GBP 1,000 trading allowance you may not need to report it. Above that, you usually register and file. Platforms such as eBay, Vinted and Etsy now report seller activity to HMRC.

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

Self-employed people and landlords move to quarterly digital reporting in stages by qualifying income: over GBP 50,000 from April 2026, over GBP 30,000 from April 2027 and over GBP 20,000 from April 2028. Check gov.uk for the current rules.

Make January easy

The best fix is records kept all year: income, expenses, mileage, and a running tax estimate so there is no surprise bill. The UK Sole Trader Self Assessment Tracker does this in Excel or Google Sheets.

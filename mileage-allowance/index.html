UK mileage allowance calculator for sole traders

If you use your own car or van for business, you can usually claim simplified mileage instead of working out actual running costs: 45p per mile for the first 10,000 business miles in the tax year, 25p per mile after that (cars and vans). Check gov.uk for current rates and for motorbikes and bicycles.

What to record for each trip

Date, start and end place, business purpose and miles. Commuting from home to a regular workplace is not normally claimable. Keep the log for at least the period HMRC requires you to keep records.

Do not double count

If you claim simplified mileage you cannot also claim actual fuel, insurance or repairs for the same vehicle.

Quarterly records for UK sole traders

General information, not tax advice. Confirm dates and rules on gov.uk.

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax is being phased in for self-employed people by income level, with quarterly updates to HMRC through compatible software. Even if it does not apply to you yet, keeping totals by quarter is a good habit: you see profit as the year goes, and the year-end return is mostly done.

The usual tax-year quarters

  • 6 April to 5 July
  • 6 July to 5 October
  • 6 October to 5 January
  • 6 January to 5 April

Some people use calendar quarters instead; check gov.uk for the reporting periods that apply to you.

What to total each quarter

  • Income received
  • Allowable expenses
  • Mileage allowance (45p / 25p per mile under simplified expenses)
  • Profit so far, and what to set aside for tax

The UK Sole Trader Self Assessment Tracker has income, expense and mileage logs with a Quarterly sheet that totals them for each tax-year quarter. It is a record-keeping aid and does not file anything with HMRC.
